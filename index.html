Engineering projects

Project Sentinel

Autonomous air-defense turret for Teknofest. Under active development.

A closed-loop turret that detects, classifies and hits balloon targets standing in for F-16s, helicopters, missiles and drones at 5, 10 and 15 metres, inside a 60 cm envelope. Developed under Vitality Technologies.

Global-shutter camera→YOLOv8 + TensorRT→10-byte UART→ESP32 / FreeRTOS→Pan/tilt gimbal
Vision
Custom YOLOv8 on an RTX 4060 at 140 to 200+ FPS, HSV friend-or-foe filtering, lead-angle calculation.
Real-time control
Core 0 parses serial and the emergency stop. Core 1 makes jitter-free STEP/DIR pulses and times the firing MOSFET.
Motion
Closed-loop steppers with 3:1 GT2 reductions. Tilt covers 0 to 60 degrees.
Fabrication
SolidWorks design, printed on a Creality K1C in ASA, PETG and PA-CF.
CAD renders and test footage will be added after testing.

C/C++ · ESP-IDF · FreeRTOS · Python · OpenCV · PyTorch · TensorRT · CUDA

Verified reviews
